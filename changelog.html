Skyline — Changelog

Every shipped version of the iOS app + the source bundle on Polar. Newest first.

v2.0.3 In review submitted 2026-05-22

  • App and App Store listing now in 15 languages — English, Vietnamese, Chinese (Simplified + Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Spanish (Spain + Mexico), Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian, Russian, Arabic (with RTL), Indonesian.
  • In-app Language picker in Settings → About; auto-detect device language on first launch.
  • Internal stability improvements.

v2.0.2 Shipped 2026-05-22

  • 3 user-feedback fixes (h/t Ann Ng):
    • Save boarding pass to Photos — long-press a flight's source photo to save
    • Sort flights by capture date rather than departure date so freshly-added passes appear at the top
    • History search (.searchable) across airline, flight number, route, dates

v2.0.1 Shipped 2026-05-21

  • Source-bundle launch — $19 on Polar (full Xcode project + AGENTS.md).
  • flownmap.com landing redesign — two-column hero, framed iPhone screenshots, source-sale section.

v2.0.0 Shipped 2026-05-17

  • Native iOS rewrite (from the previous React-Native build) — pure SwiftUI, SwiftData, SceneKit globe.
  • Apple Intelligence boarding-pass parser, with IATA-barcode verification + on-device OCR fallback.
  • ~6,000-airport IATA database baked in.
  • 3D night-lit Earth with glowing flight arcs.
  • Released on the App Store as com.devuap.skyline.

Aside (private on-device AI chat) and Pulse (Paddle revenue dashboard) are the other apps in Tony's portfolio. All three are built on the same on-device-first philosophy.
